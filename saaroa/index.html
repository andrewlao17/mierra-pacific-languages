Hla'alua

Also called Saaroa: a language of Taiwan's southern mountains

Spoken by one of Taiwan's smallest Indigenous peoples.

Who and where

A people in the mountains

  • The Hla'alua live in Taoyuan and Kaochung villages (Taoyuan District) and Maya village (Namasia District), in the mountains of Kaohsiung, southern Taiwan.
  • They are four communities: Paiciana, Vilanganʉ, Talicia and Hlihlala.
  • For about a century they were officially counted as part of the Tsou people. They campaigned to be recognized by their own name.
  • On 26 June 2014, Taiwan recognized them as its 15th Indigenous people, about 477 people (2024).
Kaohsiung, southern Taiwan

How endangered?
