Liki

An island language of Papua, Indonesia, also called Moar

Field notes: one of the least-documented languages on Earth…

Where and who

Two small islands

  • Liki is spoken on Liki and Nirumoar, small islands off the north coast of Sarmi, in Papua province, Indonesia.
  • About 320 people are Liki.
  • Its neighbourhood is crowded with languages: the Sarmi area alone has 23 Papuan and 11 Austronesian languages.
  • For everyday life, most people now use Papuan Malay, and Indonesian at school and work.
